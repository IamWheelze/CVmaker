# Quick Start Guide

## Get Started in 3 Steps

### Step 1: Open the App
```bash
# Simply open index.html in your browser
# Double-click the file or use:
open index.html
```

### Step 2: Choose Your Method

**Option A: Upload Existing Resume**
1. Click "Upload PDF Resume"
2. Select your PDF file
3. Let AI extract your information
4. Review and edit as needed

**Option B: Create from Scratch**
1. Click "Manual Input"
2. Fill in the form sections
3. Add your work experience, education, etc.

### Step 3: Generate Your CV
1. Choose a template (Modern, Professional, or Creative)
2. Click "Preview CV" to see how it looks
3. Click "Download PDF" to save your resume

## Using AI Features (Optional)

### To Enable AI:
1. Get a free API key from:
   - **Anthropic**: https://console.anthropic.com/ (recommended)
   - **OpenAI**: https://platform.openai.com/

2. Click "Improve with AI" button
3. Enter your API key when prompted
4. Save it for future use

### What AI Can Do:
- **Parse PDF resumes** automatically
- **Improve your summary** to be more professional
- **Extract structured data** from your existing CV

## Pro Tips

✅ **For PDF Upload:**
- Use text-based PDFs (not scanned images)
- Standard resume formats work best
- Review extracted data for accuracy

✅ **For Manual Input:**
- Fill in all required fields (marked with *)
- Use the "Add" buttons to include multiple experiences
- Keep your summary under 150 words

✅ **For Best Results:**
- Use action verbs in job descriptions
- Quantify achievements with numbers
- List skills relevant to your target job
- Proofread before downloading

## No Installation Needed!

This is a standalone web application:
- ✅ No downloads or installations
- ✅ No account creation
- ✅ No data collection
- ✅ Works offline (except AI features)
- ✅ Your data stays private
